Mission Briefing — TL;DR
  • The game underneath the technical mess is genuinely excellent — critics agree (85 MC).
  • The 22% of negative Steam reviews are about performance, not gameplay.
  • 73K peak players = #2 Star Wars Steam launch ever. The audience is there; patches will decide retention.

Who should buy, who should wait

✓ Buy it now if you…

  • Loved XCOM 2 — this is its Star Wars heir, from ex-Firaxis devs
  • Want a story-first Star Wars with no lightsaber power fantasy
  • Have an RTX 3070-class GPU or better (or play on PS5/XSX)
  • Enjoy squad bonding & permadeath tension

⏸ Wait for a patch if you…

  • Run a GTX 1080-class card or 16GB RAM
  • Play on Steam Deck (35–55 FPS, needs tuning)
  • Are sensitive to stutter — UE5 shader hitching is real
  • Want the crashes fixed (Patch 1.02 promised)

✕ Skip it if you…

  • Expect Jedi: Survivor-style action — this is slow, tactical, grid-based
  • Hate percentage-to-hit RNG (it's XCOM DNA, miss chances included)
  • Only play multiplayer — this is 100% single-player
⚠ Performance Warning

Before you buy, check your rig against the real requirements — the official "minimum" spec is optimistic. If you're borderline, our performance guide can claw back ~40 FPS with the right settings, but we won't pretend the launch build is clean.

Will the Steam rating keep recovering?
Likely yes — the trajectory (69% → 78% in 72 hours) matches other UE5 launches where day-1 performance reviews get diluted as patches land. The gameplay reviews are overwhelmingly positive.
How long is the campaign?
Community-verified ~40 hours for the main campaign of 24 missions and 4 boss fights, longer if you run optional Operations.
Is it on Game Pass or EA Play?
Not at launch. It's an EA published title, so an EA Play arrival is plausible in 6–12 months, but nothing is confirmed.
