Intel FAQ

Quick answers

The questions every new commander asks first.

When does Star Wars Zero Company release, and on what platforms?
It launched August 27, 2026 on PC (Steam), PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Standard Edition is $49.99, Deluxe is $59.99.
Is permadeath really in the game?
Yes, but it's forgiving: an operative only dies permanently after being downed 3 times without healing between missions. You'll get plenty of warning.
How big is my squad, and can I respec characters?
You field 4 operatives per mission. Hawks and most recruits can fully respec between missions — Medic today, Sniper tomorrow.
Why is my frame rate so bad — and how do I fix it?
The known day-1 culprit is Environment Geometry Detail. Disabling it recovers ~40 FPS for many players. See our full performance guide for the complete settings table and crash fixes.
Is it worth $49.99 with the mixed reviews?
Steam ratings have recovered from 69% to 78% as patches land. XCOM fans should buy now; players on older GPUs may want to wait. Full verdict here.
