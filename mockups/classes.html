Best Class for Beginners: Medic

The 3-down permadeath rule sounds forgiving until a mission goes sideways and two operatives are bleeding out. A Medic doesn't just heal — they reset the permadeath clock, which makes them the single highest-value slot in your first campaign. Pair with a Sharpshooter and let the authored characters (Trick, Cly, Tel-Rea) carry the damage while you learn.

Secret & Exotic Classes

Four exotic classes sit outside the standard eight — Jedi, Mandalorian skirmisher, Astromech and one unlockable late-campaign class we're still verifying (no spoilers in the URL slugs). These are tied to authored characters rather than hired recruits, which means losing them to permadeath costs you the class entirely. Build your Bonds early.
