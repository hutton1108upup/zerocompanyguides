The Handbook's position · updated Aug 30
Is permadeath really in the game?
Yes, but forgiving: an operative dies permanently only after 3 downs without healing between missions.
How long is the campaign?
Community-verified ~40 hours across 24 missions and 4 boss fights, plus optional Operations.
Standard or Deluxe edition?
Standard. Deluxe ($59.99) adds cosmetics only — two skin packs and 5 weapon paints.
